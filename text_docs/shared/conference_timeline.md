*Scoring columns (judged 2026-07-16; revisit when research direction shifts). **Rep (0–10)** = the venue's OWN reputation/prestige, paper-agnostic — the dominant consideration when choosing where to submit. For workshops it combines the parent-conference brand, the series' maturity/track record, and the fact that workshop papers are typically NON-archival (rep ceiling well below main conferences: even the strongest workshop series sits ~~6). Anchors: 10 = NeurIPS-class main track · 9 = AAAI/ICLR class · 7–8 = strong established conference · 6–6.5 = respected young/niche conference or the top workshop series · 4 = typical first-edition NeurIPS workshop · ≤3.5 = narrow-pool or non-research venue. **Research fit (0–10)** = topical fit to this repo's research lines (jailbreak attack/defense evaluation, LLM-judge reliability, VLM safety; quantum-ML side line) — secondary to Rep, tie-breaks among comparable venues and gates what is submittable at all (Research fit ≤2 = would not submit regardless of Rep). **Bar (0–10)** = acceptance difficulty on ONE unified scale across both tables: 9 ≈ top conference main track (~~20% accept, strongest pool) · 7 ≈ strong conference / Findings-backed cycle · 5–6 ≈ competitive established workshop series · 3–4 ≈ typical first-edition workshop · ≤2 ≈ accepts most reasonable on-topic submissions. First-edition workshop bars have no history — treat ±1.*

*Submission-logic columns (verified 2026-07-20 from venue CFPs + NeurIPS workshop guidance). ONE pairing rule sits behind all of them: a paper may sit at several venues at once only if EVERY venue holding it allows the pairing — each venue's clause opens or closes only its OWN door, and the strictest clause wins (a workshop saying "under-review work welcome" cannot waive another venue's ban). The columns record each venue's doors:*

- ***Archival** — is the paper "used up" by acceptance? archival = it becomes a formal publication, no future submission anywhere; non-archival = still free to go to an archival venue later.*
- ***Dual-submission clause** (conference table) — what that conference allows WHILE the paper is under review there. The ML norm: no other ARCHIVAL venue, but arXiv + non-archival workshops explicitly OK — so conference + workshop pairs are normally legal.*
- ***Accepts under-review / published?** (workshop table) — the workshop's inbound door. The norm: work under review elsewhere is welcome; work already PUBLISHED at an ML venue is excluded.*
- ***Multi-workshop?** (workshop table) — same paper at several workshops at once. Per-workshop choice; NeurIPS has NO central rule about it.*

*"not stated" = the CFP is silent on that door — re-verify before relying on it.*

*Remote-presentation column (verified 2026-07-21 from official venue policy pages) — can an accepted paper be presented **remotely/virtually**, or must a presenting author attend in person? Values answer that directly: **yes** = a virtual/online presentation track is offered · **no — in-person only** = at least one author must present in person (usually a no-show removal policy, no remote option) · **unknown** = no general policy published for this edition (only case-by-case visa/hardship exceptions, if any). Rows for future editions without their own CFP inherit the venue's most recent settled policy (year cited in-cell) — re-verify when the edition's CFP lands. This is a travel/attendance-planning aid, orthogonal to the submission-logic columns above.*
