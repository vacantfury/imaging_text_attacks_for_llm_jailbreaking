**Namespacing convention.** Each paper owns a subdir keyed by its **Namespace** above under `text_docs/`,
`conf/experiment/`, and `outputs/`; `shared/` holds cross-paper material (venue facts, judge validation,
literature, this index). Series IDs are the primary identifiers; the letters (A/B/C/D/E) remain valid
aliases and the historical shorthand; codenames are the paper-facing titles. When a new paper starts, it is
born ID-first — add its row here and create its namespace subdirs. Note: the letter E was reassigned
2026-08-02 to the model-internals first paper (**AS-5**, repo `model_internals_safety`); a pre-08-02
"Paper E" in this repo's docs means Smuggled Actions (dead 2026-08-02 — dead papers carry no series ID).

**Note — AS-2 and AS-7 each SPLIT AGAIN on 2026-08-22 (owner-ratified), by the one-quantity test.** Both drafts had accumulated material that measures a DIFFERENT quantity from the one their thesis is about, which is what made both feel unfocused. The test applied to every inclusion: *does this measure the paper's quantity, or a different one?* Same quantity in a supporting role goes to supplementary; a different quantity becomes the next paper. **AS-2's quantity** is the refusal shift caused by an uninformative attachment; its five characterised properties (who pays, does it correct, does it need an image, is the sign stable, does it track available harm) are one paper, and the CARRIER of the magnitude left as **AS-9**. **AS-7's quantity** is the guardrail's own share of the refusals credited to it (0% / 41–45% / 99% across three settings a results table would describe identically); both the attacker's channel choice and the evaluator's read choice move that one number, so AS-7 is NOT halved — what left is the DESIGN response (routed panel, stacking, coverage, efficacy), as **AS-8**. AS-7's spine moves from "read access" (a taxonomy of causes, layered on top) to the share itself (the measured effect); note this is neither of the two earlier titles — the first led with the instrument, the second with the causes, and the fix is to lead with the number. The two papers shipping now are the two MEASUREMENT papers, one at the model layer and one at the guard layer; the two founded are the EXPLANATION and DESIGN papers.
